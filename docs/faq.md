# FAQ

## What if my ancestry, background, or class is missing?

- **Custom ancestry.** With **Standard ancestry boosts**, fill in any fixed boosts and the ancestry flaw from your source, then choose the free boosts shown. Leave unused fixed boost or flaw fields at **None**. If you're using the alternate ancestry rule, select **Alternate — two free boosts**.
- **Custom background.** The calculator offers two boost slots. Use the attributes your background grants, including any restrictions listed in its rules.
- **Custom class.** Under **Choose a key attribute**, select the attribute granted by your class or subclass. Check your source for the class's feat schedule.

## How do I enter Raised by Belief?

Select **Raised by Belief** under **Background**. In **Divine attribute**, choose one attribute from your deity's Divine Attribute entry. The calculator offers all six attributes in this slot, so check your deity's entry before choosing. In **Free background boost**, choose a different attribute. For example, if your deity lists Wisdom or Charisma, you can pick Wisdom first and Dexterity second. See *Player Core*, p. 88.

At **Feat level 1**, the planner shows **Assurance (your deity’s divine skill)** under **Granted by background**. Record the deity and the specific skill on your character sheet.

## Why does my background show one or three boosts?

**Feral Child** has one boost, chosen from Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. **Amnesiac** has two boosts you choose and a third labeled **GM’s boost**. Leave the third slot empty until your GM tells you which attribute to enter. See *Player Core 2*, p. 52.

## How do I record a feat that's missing from the picker?

Set **Feat level** to the level where you gain the feat. In the appropriate slot, select **Choose feat**, open **Enter a feat by name**, type its name, and select **Use feat**. For a feat granted by a custom background, use the **Background feat** slot at level 1. Recorded feat names are included in share links and JSON backups.
