Player Core rules

How boosts work

  1. Start each modifier at +0. Apply your ancestry, background, and class boosts, then choose four different attributes to boost at level 1.
  2. At levels 5, 10, 15, and 20, choose four different attributes again.
  3. From +4 onward, two boosts at different milestones raise the modifier by 1.
